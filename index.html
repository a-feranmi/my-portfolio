    My story

    From reports to the pipelines behind them

    I follow one rule with data: don't trust a result until you've checked how it was measured.

    Since 2023 I've worked on client engagements through two consulting firms, Bredge LLC and Hillfield Interactive. I started as a Power BI developer, building dashboards, semantic models and workspaces from scratch.

    Over time I kept finding that the hardest problems sat before the dashboard: tables nobody trusted, slow queries, numbers that didn't reconcile. So I moved down the stack. Today I rebuild raw and staging layers in BigQuery, run dbt pipelines, set up data-quality monitoring, and analyse A/B tests.

    My MSc in Data Science Management, finished with a distinction, added machine learning and full-stack builds. Next I'm going deeper into data engineering with orchestration and large-scale processing.
